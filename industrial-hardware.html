Hardware FAQs

Do you supply the hardware, or just the software?

We provide turnkey solutions. We supply, install, and integrate all hardware (Kiosks, Boom Barriers, Sensors) along with the software. However, if you already have compatible hardware, we can integrate with it.

How is the hardware protected from lightning or power surges?

All sensitive equipment is routed through industrial-grade opto-isolators and surge protection devices. We strictly adhere to grounding best practices during installation.
