Complete Protection

Detect. Prevent. Secure.

Our multi-layered security protocol uses hardware sensors and software logic to eliminate manipulation at the weighbridge.

Position Sensors

Infrared beams ensure the truck is completely on the platform. If a tire is hanging off the edge, the system refuses to lock the weight.

Live Snapshots

Integration with up to 4 IP cameras. The exact moment the weight is locked, photos of the front, rear, and cabin are saved to the database.

Immutable Logs

Every deleted ticket, manual weight entry, or reprinted slip is permanently logged and triggers an instant SMS alert to management.

The "Invisible Guard" Engine

Our AI-powered guard engine constantly analyzes incoming weight streams for anomalies, even when the operator is not looking.

Security FAQs

Can the operator bypass the position sensors?

No. The software is hardcoded to require a "CLEAR" signal from the PLC before the "Save Ticket" button is enabled. Only an administrator with a master password can bypass this in an emergency.

What happens if a camera fails?

The system can be configured to block weighment if cameras are offline, or allow weighment but flag the ticket as "NO_IMAGE" and send an alert to management regarding hardware failure.
