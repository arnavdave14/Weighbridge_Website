# Website Content Requirements Checklist

This document outlines all the text, images, and content required from the client to complete the Aaravya Enterprises website. Please review each page and provide the necessary content for the respective sections.

---

## 1. Global Elements (Across All Pages)
These elements appear on every page of the website and only need to be provided once.

*   **Company Logo:** High-resolution logo (SVG or transparent PNG preferred).
*   **Contact Information:**
    *   Primary Phone Number
    *   Primary Email Address
    *   Physical Office Address(es)
*   **Social Media Links:** URLs for LinkedIn, Twitter, Facebook, YouTube, etc.
*   **Footer Details:**
    *   Brief company description for the footer.
    *   Any specific legal disclaimers or copyright text.

---

## 2. Home Page (`index.html`)
The main landing page representing the company's core value proposition.

*   **Hero Section:**
    *   Main Headline (Catchy, establishing authority).
    *   Sub-headline (Brief description of what you do).
    *   Call-to-Action (CTA) button text (e.g., "Get a Quote", "Explore Solutions").
*   **About/Intro Section:** A short paragraph explaining who Aaravya Enterprises is and your core mission.
*   **Key Features/Benefits:** 3-4 key selling points (e.g., "100% Accuracy", "Zero Fraud"). Provide a short title and 1-2 sentences for each.
*   **Client Logos (Trusted By):** High-resolution logos of your current top clients or partners.
*   **Testimonials / Client Success:** 3-5 reviews from clients. Provide:
    *   Client Name & Company
    *   The review text
    *   Client photo (optional but recommended)
*   **Gallery / Portfolio:** 4-6 images showing your weighbridges, hardware, and control rooms in action. Provide titles and brief descriptions for each image.
*   **Call-to-Action (Bottom):** Final push for the user to contact you. Headline and button text.

---

## 3. Product & Service Pages
For each of the detailed service pages below, we need the following standard structure of content:
1.  **Main Headline & Sub-headline:** What is this specific product/service?
2.  **Introduction/Overview:** 1-2 paragraphs explaining the solution.
3.  **Key Features Grid:** 3-6 specific features. Need a title and a short description for each.
4.  **Benefits / "Why Choose Us":** Why is this solution better than competitors?
5.  **Gallery Images:** 3 high-quality images showcasing this specific service, along with titles and short descriptions for the zoom-in lightbox.
6.  **FAQs (Optional but recommended):** 3-5 Frequently Asked Questions and their answers related to this specific service.

**Please provide the above structure for EACH of the following pages:**

*   **Weighbridge Software (`weighbridge-software.html`)**
    *   Focus: Desktop/web software for weighing operations.
*   **Industrial Hardware (`industrial-hardware.html`)**
    *   Focus: Sensors, boom barriers, traffic lights, RFID readers, cameras.
*   **ERP Integration (`erp-integration.html`)**
    *   Focus: SAP, Oracle, Tally, and custom API integrations.
*   **Cloud Dashboard (`cloud-dashboard.html`)**
    *   Focus: Real-time cloud reporting and analytics from anywhere.
*   **Manless Operations (`manless.html`)**
    *   Focus: Fully automated, driver-operated weighbridge kiosks.
*   **Zero Fraud System (`zero-fraud.html`)**
    *   Focus: Anti-theft mechanisms, positioning sensors, CCTV syncing.
*   **Crusher Management (`crusher-management.html`)**
    *   Focus: Specialized software for stone crushers and mining.
*   **Inventory & Warehouse (`inventory-warehouse.html`)**
    *   Focus: Tracking stock, logistics, and gate-pass management.
*   **Custom Software Development (`custom-software.html`)**
    *   Focus: Tailor-made software solutions for enterprise clients.
*   **Office Management (`office-management.html`)**
    *   Focus: Internal tools for HR, payroll, and back-office tracking.
*   **Website Development (`website-development.html`)**
    *   Focus: Corporate websites and web portals.
*   **Mobile App Development (`mobile-app-development.html`)**
    *   Focus: Android and iOS app development services.

---

## 4. Privacy Policy & Legal (`privacy.html`)
*   **Privacy Policy Text:** The full legal text regarding data collection, cookies, and user privacy.
*   **Terms of Service (Optional):** If required, full legal text outlining the terms of using your software/services.

---

## 5. Contact Section / Forms
*   **Form Fields:** Confirm what information you need to collect from visitors (e.g., Name, Email, Phone, Company, Message).
*   **Success Message:** The text to display after a user successfully submits the contact form.

---

### Instructions for the Client:
Please provide the content in a structured format (e.g., a Word document or Google Doc) clearly labeled with the corresponding Page and Section names above. For images, please provide them in a labeled ZIP file or a shared Google Drive folder.
