Web Development FAQs

Do you provide domain name and hosting?

Yes, we can manage the entire lifecycle including domain registration, SSL certificates, business emails (Google Workspace), and enterprise-grade CDN hosting.

Will I be able to edit the text and images myself?

Absolutely. We integrate a headless CMS (Content Management System) that allows your marketing team to easily swap images, post blogs, and update text without needing a developer.

How long does it take to design and build a corporate website?

A standard 5-10 page corporate website usually takes 3 to 4 weeks from initial wireframes to final deployment. More complex sites with custom portals or e-commerce can take 8-12 weeks.
