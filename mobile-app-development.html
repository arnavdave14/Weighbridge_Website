Mobile App FAQs

Should I build Native or Cross-Platform?

If your app relies heavily on hardware (Bluetooth integration, heavy camera usage, complex 3D rendering), go Native. If you are building a B2B dashboard, e-commerce, or utility app and need to launch on both stores quickly and cost-effectively, React Native or Flutter is the best choice.

Can you update the app after it's in the store?

Yes, we provide ongoing maintenance contracts to ensure your app stays updated with the latest iOS/Android OS versions, fix bugs, and deploy new features seamlessly via Over-The-Air (OTA) updates when applicable.

How long does an app take to build?

A simple MVP (Minimum Viable Product) cross-platform app can take 6-8 weeks. A complex, feature-rich native application with a custom backend can take anywhere from 3 to 6 months.
