Core Protection

Zero-Fraud Weighment System

Manual weighbridges lose you money every shift — ghost trips, inflated tare weights, manipulated slips. Aaravya removes the operator from the equation. Weight is locked by hardware, not humans. Manipulation becomes structurally impossible.

Position sensor — weight locks only when truck is fully on platform
CCTV image captured automatically at every single weighment
Tamper-proof audit trail — every edit logged with operator ID & timestamp
RFID & ANPR driver identity verification at every gate entry
