Zero-Fraud Weighment System

Manual weighbridges lose you money every shift - through ghost trips, inflated tare weights, and manipulated slips. Aaravya removes the operator from the equation entirely. The weight is locked by hardware, not by a human. Manipulation becomes structurally impossible.

  • Position sensor - weight locks only when the truck is fully on the platform
  • CCTV image captured automatically at every weighment
  • Tamper-proof audit trail - every edit logged with operator ID
  • RFID and ANPR driver identity verification

SAP, ERP & Portal Integration

One weighment. Data flows instantly to SAP, Tally, Oracle, or the Annadarpan government portal - automatically, with zero manual re-entry at any step.

ANPR Camera & RFID

The system reads the truck's number plate or RFID card automatically. No driver needs to do anything. Vehicle identified, weight recorded, barrier opens - under 60 seconds.

Cloud Dashboard

Live weighment data from every site, on your phone or desktop - anywhere in India or abroad, any time.

Standard Weighbridge Software

Works with any existing weighbridge - any machine from brands like Avery, Mettler, Essae, CAS. Gross, tare and net weight recording, GST invoice, slip printing, and multi-user LAN support. Ready in hours, not weeks.

Manless Weighbridge System

No operator. No shift. No manipulation. RFID or ANPR identifies the truck, position sensor locks the weight, boom barrier opens - under 60 seconds, 24 hours a day.

RFID & ANPR Integration

Vehicle walks in, system already knows who it is. RFID card or ANPR camera identifies the truck instantly - no driver action, no manual number entry, no room for fraud at the gate.
