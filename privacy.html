Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 2026

1. Introduction

Aaravya Enterprises ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website or use our weighbridge automation systems.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect information that identifies you or your business, including:

  • Contact Data: Name, business email, phone number, and mailing address.
  • Operational Data: Vehicle numbers, weighbridge transaction logs, and driver identification (where integrated with our ANPR or RFID systems).
  • Technical Data: IP address, browser type, and usage patterns collected via cookies and similar technologies.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected data for various purposes, including:

  • Providing and maintaining our automation ecosystem.
  • Syncing weighbridge data with your ERP systems (SAP, Oracle, etc.).
  • Detecting and preventing weighbridge fraud and theft.
  • Sending technical updates and responding to support requests.

4. Data Security

We implement state-of-the-art administrative, technical, and physical security measures to protect your operational data. However, no electronic transmission over the Internet or information storage technology can be guaranteed to be 100% secure.

5. Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell your personal or operational data. We may share information with trusted third-party service providers (like cloud infrastructure partners) only as necessary to provide our services.
