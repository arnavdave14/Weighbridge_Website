Inventory FAQs

Does this replace my existing inventory software?

It can act as a standalone lightweight inventory tracker, but it is designed to seamlessly push data to your dedicated ERP (like Tally or SAP) rather than entirely replacing it.

Can I track multiple godowns/warehouses?

Yes, you can configure multiple storage locations. The operator selects the destination godown during weighment, and the stock is allocated accordingly.
