How Manless Weighing Works.

A completely touchless, operator-free workflow that accelerates throughput and eliminates human error.

1. RFID Tag Registration

The system scans for an RFID tag. If unregistered, the process exits. If verified, the entry boom barrier opens and the traffic light signals the vehicle to enter.

2. Automated Audio Guidance

Automated audio plays to guide the vehicle inside the ultrasonic sensor zone, then provides precise instructions to set the vehicle perfectly on the deck.

3. Manual Driver Confirmation

Once aligned, the system announces in the selected language asking the driver to step out and manually press the physical push button to confirm.

4. Weighment & Image Capture

The system immediately records the gross/tare weight and captures security images. The recorded weight is instantly shown on the LED display.

5. Automated Exit

The exit boom barrier opens and a final audio message plays: "Please exit your vehicle, thank you," completing the weighment cycle.

Anti-Fraud Lockout

If the positioning sensors detect a wheel is off the platform, or if multiple vehicles try to weigh together, the system physically locks out. The barrier stays down, and an alert is sent to security.

Multi-Lingual Voice

Built-in PA integration plays automated audio prompts in the local language (e.g. Hindi, English, Marathi). "Please move forward", "Weighment complete, collect slip".

Automation FAQs

What happens if a driver needs help?

The driver kiosk includes an intercom/SIP phone button. Pressing it connects them directly to central security or the remote weighbridge admin.

Can we use our existing boom barriers?

Yes! If your existing barriers have standard NO/NC dry relay contacts, our relay controller board can easily integrate with them.
