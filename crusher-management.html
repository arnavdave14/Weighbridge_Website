Crusher FAQs

Can it handle multiple aggregate sizes?

Yes, you can configure unlimited material masters (10mm, 20mm, Dust, GSB, etc.) and generate production and sales reports per material type.

How does the royalty pass system work?

Depending on your state's portal capabilities, we either integrate directly via API to fetch pass details, or allow operators to scan the QR code on the pass to link it automatically to the weighment slip.
